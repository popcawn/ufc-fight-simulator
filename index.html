How to read this

Fair win % blends the market with the model. EARLY = the line first appeared under 72h ago and hasn't moved 3+ pts, so it uses opening-line weights: the backtested rule (+8–9% ROI, beat the closing line 70%). LATE = fight-week weights: smaller edges, promising in the backtest (+6%) but not proven. TAKE = fair % beats the best available price by 5–20% (smaller edges were noise in the backtest; bigger ones were usually the model being wrong). Total rounds (the one prop in the sportsbook feed) shows the market's odds next to the model's, for information only. The model's round timing is calibrated on ~2,000 past fights but much blunter than the books, and there's no prop-line history to prove an edge, so it's never flagged as a bet. Hover a fight's call to see it without the toggle. Method props (KO/sub/decision, double chance) aren't in the feed: click Sim and type them into the calculator. Moneylines only, the one market with a proven edge. Stakes are % of bankroll (¼-Kelly, max 3%, halved when a fighter has under 4 UFC fights). Sim opens the fight with these prices filled in.

My bets

CLV (closing-line value) = your price vs the de-vigged consensus line at fight time. Positive means you beat the market's final number, the fastest proof of a real edge (about 50 bets, vs hundreds for win/loss). It shows "live" until the fight starts. Right after you bet it is usually slightly negative (you just paid the book's cut); it turns positive when the line moves your way by fight time. Units: 1u = 1% of bankroll. Click W / L / P to settle. Saved in this browser only: Export on one computer, Import on another.

Track record — model vs the betting market

How the odds calculator works

Type a sportsbook's American odds (e.g. -150 or +220) for any outcome. The calculator anchors to the betting market: it blends the moneyline you enter with the model, weighted by when you are betting (the model moves an opening line more than a fight-week one). In a 2021–26 backtest against real opening/closing lines, raw model edges lost money while the anchored blend made money. Fair % = the probability behind fair odds & EV — the raw model until you enter both moneylines, then the market-anchored blend (shown in gold) · Mkt % = the book's no-vig price. Green = positive EV, ★ = best single bet, ⚠ suspect = the verdict says pass.

Honesty note: no fight model hits 90% — betting favorites win roughly 65–69% of UFC fights. On its own this model picks ~66% of winners on 741 held-out fights (Jan 2025 → now, pre-fight data only, no leakage), but the closing betting line is sharper still. What the backtest shows is that the model knows things the opening line doesn't — the line moves toward it before fight night — and that anchoring the model to the market is what turns that into a betting edge. That's why the calculator anchors to the line. Every number here is walk-forward: each prediction made before the fight, with the model refit only on earlier years. Past results don't guarantee future ones — expect losing cards.
