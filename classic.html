About Classic v1

Reading the table

  • Fair is the model's price with no book margin. Market is the book's chance with its margin removed (on the two-way win and distance markets). EV is your expected return per dollar at the odds you typed (e.g. -150 or +220).
  • Best is the one row to bet if you only bet one. Value rows are the same fight, so treat them as one position. When the verdict says pass, positive rows are marked suspect.
  • Props are estimated from the simulation's method and round mix.
  • Model-based suggestion, not financial advice. Bet only what you can afford to lose.

The model

  • Monte Carlo engine + trained outcome model (Elo, age, reach, form, ring rust, experience, octagon control), 10,000 simulated fights per matchup.
  • 65.8% on 585 held-out real fights. Method and round mix calibrated to the real 2018+ finish distribution.
  • This is the original Sept 28 model and betting rules, unchanged. Only the page layout is new. Classic 2.0 keeps the same win probability and re-tests the method split and the rules against real prices.
